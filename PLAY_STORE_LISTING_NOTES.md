## Full Description Draft

GTOOL X is a document and productivity utility application designed to help you manage, view, process, and organize your files right on your device.

### Key Features:
- **PDF Tools**: View, merge, split, compress, and edit PDF documents locally.
- **Image Resizer & Presets**: Crop and resize photos with standard dimension presets for applications, passports, and documents.
- **Local OCR Text Extraction**: Extract selectable text from scanned documents and images using local machine learning technology.
- **Smart Invoice Maker**: Create invoices and bills with automatic totals and itemized breakdowns.
- **Notes & Universal Search**: Keep notes and perform fast, indexed searches across your local documents and text.
- **Task Reminders**: Schedule exact local alarms and reminders for important due dates.
- **Biometric Lock**: Protect access to your local workspace using Android fingerprint or face unlock.
- **Local Backup & Restore**: Export and import backup archives using Android's safe file picker.
